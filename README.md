# INFN-Intership
Characterization and qualification of devices and test structures from the pre-production of sensors for the Endcap Timing Layer detector of the CMS experiment.

## HPK QC-TS
The goal is to validate the manufacturer's analysis on the HPK pre-series test structures. After performing the CV characteristic measurements, the value of V_gl is extracted. It is defined as the intersection point of the lines describing the linearity in the bulk region and in the gain region.

## FPK Charge Stability
The objective of the analysis is to verify that the charge generated in the sensor by a beta particle (using a beta radiation source) remains stable over time. The original .root file is not shown due to its large size.
