# INFN-Intership
Characterization and qualification of devices and test structures from the pre-production of sensors for the Endcap Timing Layer detector of the CMS experiment.

## HPK QC-TS
The goal is to validate the manufacturer's analysis on the HPK pre-series test structures. After performing the CV characteristic measurements, the value of V_gl is extracted. It is defined as the intersection point of the lines describing the linearity in the bulk region and in the gain region. Determining the optimal range for the linear fit in the gain region is a crucial step. Finally, the histogram of the V_gl values is compared with the vendor's data.

## FPK Charge Stability
The objective of the analysis is to verify that the charge generated in the sensor by a beta particle (using a beta radiation source) remains stable over time. The charge value is computed by dividing the signal area within a specific time window by a known resistance value of the setup. This area corresponds to the Most Probable Value (MPV) extracted from a Landau fit applied to the area distribution in the considered time range. The .root file is not shown due to its large size.
